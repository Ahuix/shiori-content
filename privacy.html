Shiori privacy policy

Last updated 11 October 2026

Shiori is a Japanese reading app made by Henry Xie. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app or its web version.

We don't collect your data

Shiori has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. We don't collect, store or share any personal information.

What stays on your device

Your reading progress, bookmarks, study list and settings are saved only on your device (in the app, or in your browser for the web version). They are never sent to us. Deleting the app, or clearing your browser's data, removes them.

Downloads

New stories, recorded voices and background sounds are plain files downloaded from our website, which is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like any website, GitHub may keep standard technical logs such as IP addresses; see GitHub's privacy statement. The web version also loads a font from Google Fonts.

Children

Shiori is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page. Questions: xzh_sz@hotmail.com.
