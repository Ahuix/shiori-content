Shiori support

Questions, ideas, or found a word that's read wrongly? Email xzh_sz@hotmail.com. Please mention the story and episode if it's about a particular sentence.

I can't hear anything

Turn the volume up. In the app, sound plays even with the silent switch on. In a web browser on iPhone, tap ▶ once to start the sound; browsers only allow sound after a tap.

New stories

New stories download by themselves when the app starts (it needs an internet connection). You can also check in Settings → Stories.

Try it in a browser

The web version is at ahuix.github.io/shiori-content.
